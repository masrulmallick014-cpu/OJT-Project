PulseCorp Account & Personal Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your PulseCorp account and all associated personal and health data either directly inside the mobile app or by submitting a request below.

Option 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the PulseCorp app and tap Profile in the top-right corner.
  2. Tap Delete Account & Data.
  3. Confirm by tapping Delete Permanently.

What Data Is Permanently Deleted

  • Your account profile (name, work email, department, body calibration metrics, and session).
  • All stored health and wellness records (daily steps, distance, active energy, hydration logs, mindful minutes, heart rate, and sleep summaries).
  • Your HR health-sharing consent records and optional company ID card submission.
  • All community posts, replies, activity proposals, blocks, and content reports authored by your account.
